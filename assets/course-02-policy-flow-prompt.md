## 提示词

+Use case: scientific-educational. A wide 16:9 friendly illustrated infographic for a Chinese beginner finance course. Input image is ONLY reference for visual style and the Congmu founder couple. Simple polished 2D cartoon, cream and muted sage background, teal outlines, mustard and coral accents, no realism or 3D. Recurring romantic couple: tousled dark-haired man in teal overshirt and cream trousers; woman with shoulder-length dark hair, mustard shirt and cream overalls.
Four distinct story stations in one horizontal row, same size, spacious rounded cream panels with large scene illustrations (not icons alone). Unobstructed coral arrows between each station pointing right. These are conceptual transmission links, not direct money transfers. No return arrows, no suggestion of automatic results or stock price forecasts.
Station 1: a central bank building with classical columns, neutral environment/settings symbols, NOT an ordinary customer loan desk. Heading exactly "央行与金融环境" (may wrap across two lines). Small text exactly "政策利率、准备金、流动性".
Station 2: a different modern commercial bank interior, a banker considering a blank loan folder at a desk, no currency handed out. Heading exactly "商业银行". Small text exactly "资金成本与信贷决策".
Station 3: clothing workshop, Congmu founder couple discussing fabric rolls, a sewing machine and a blank company ledger; visible mustard T-shirt. Heading exactly "聪沐". Small text exactly "借款、布料、工资与定价".
Station 4: ordinary customer holding a shopping bag with a T-shirt and a separate workshop employee in apron checking a blank pay slip, NOT the founder couple. Heading exactly "客户与员工". Small text exactly "消费、收入与购买力".
Large clean legible correctly spelled Chinese labels; no additional text, no watermarks, no financial data or numbers, no big title. All characters and scenes fit inside canvas, generous breathing room. Show different institutions and decisions rather than identical abstract boxes. Accurate flow order left to right is central bank environment -> commercial bank -> Congmu -> households/employees.
